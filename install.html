Install the StreamBox Web App

Adds StreamBox to your Home Screen with its own full-screen window - no Safari tabs, no URL bar. Works in about two minutes.

  1. Open StreamBox in Safari (this page, right now).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow, in the Safari toolbar).
  3. Scroll and select Add to Home Screen.
  4. Enable Open as Web App if your iOS version offers it.
  5. Tap Add.
  6. Launch StreamBox from your Home Screen - done.
Honest limits of the web app on iOS: Picture-in-Picture and AirPlay are limited by Safari; playback runs through the same players as the website; offline covers the app shell only (streaming needs internet). Your Continue Watching, My List and settings stay on your device.

Native app - beta via AltStore / SideStore

The native iPhone + iPad app (built by this repository's CI on every change) can be installed for free by re-signing it with your own Apple ID. Runs on Windows or macOS - no Mac development machine needed for the free path.

  1. Install AltStore (Windows/Mac) or SideStore on your device.
  2. Download the latest streambox-ios-unsigned-device.ipa artifact from the GitHub Actions run (link below).
  3. Open the IPA with AltStore/SideStore - it re-signs with your Apple ID and installs.
  4. Free provisioning expires after 7 days: refresh weekly (AltStore refreshes on your Wi-Fi; SideStore can refresh anywhere).
Get the IPA from CI Full free-install guide
The IPA is not directly installable. iOS only installs apps signed by Apple-issued certificates - that is Apple's rule, not ours. AltStore/SideStore do the legitimate re-signing with your free Apple ID. Never trust anyone claiming an unsigned IPA installs directly.

Troubleshooting

I don't see "Add to Home Screen" in Safari

Make sure you opened this page in Safari itself (not the in-app browser of another app). In-app browsers hide that option. Tap the Share button in Safari's toolbar - the option is inside the share sheet, you may need to scroll down.

The Home Screen icon opens in a Safari tab instead of its own window

Remove the icon, reopen the site in Safari, and re-add it. If your iOS shows an "Open as Web App" toggle in the Add dialog, enable it before tapping Add.

Video does not play on iPhone/iPad

Some providers serve players that only work in desktop browsers - StreamBox shows a clear fallback message and offers another mirror. Tap the server/mirror button under the player to switch sources. Report the title on GitHub and we will look at it.

The native beta stopped opening after 7 days

That is free Apple-ID provisioning expiring - refresh the app from AltStore/SideStore (needs your Apple ID sign-in again) or re-install the IPA. Continue Watching and My List survive reinstalls because they live in the app's web storage.

Is any of this against Apple's rules?

No. Add to Home Screen is Apple's own feature. AltStore/SideStore use Apple's free personal-signing program exactly as designed (with its real 7-day limit). We never use enterprise certificates or signing bypasses, and we will not ship anything that does.
