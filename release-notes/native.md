Stability and polish pass: the episodes sheet can browse other seasons without disturbing what plays, finished titles no longer creep back into Continue Watching, and the D-pad timeline scrub lands where you left it. Plus fixes across the party hub and the update flow.

What is new
- The Episodes sheet browses seasons on its own: step S-/S+ through the catalog while your episode keeps playing - only tapping a card switches what plays
- Rewatching a finished movie or episode starts it fresh (the old resume used to drop you on the last seconds of the credits)

Fixes
- The D-pad timeline scrub now seeks to exactly where the slider stopped (it used to lag one step behind or skip the seek entirely)
- Finished titles no longer reappear in Continue Watching at 99% when you back out of the player
- Auto-next can no longer walk into an episode that does not exist or has not aired when the episode list fails to load
- A late Up Next from a closing player can never double-step past the episode just opened
- Rapid double-presses on Next Episode skip exactly one episode, not two
- Browsing seasons in the Episodes sheet no longer rewrites your save point onto the wrong episode
- A corrupt saved-subtitle entry can no longer silently drop your latest subtitle pick
- An interrupted update download never leaves a partial APK in storage
- The Watch Party hub now limits room creation per source and caps total rooms (one noisy client can no longer exhaust the hub for everyone)
- Party chat size limits now count real bytes, and stale rate-limit state is swept on disconnect
- A failed catalog lookup no longer caches an empty Anime area for an hour
- The title page loads its details and trailer chips race-free (trailer/cert no longer get dropped when details are slow)
- Browse paging no longer spills a slow page into a filter you just changed
- Long session memory is now bounded in Browse (cache evicts its oldest entries)
