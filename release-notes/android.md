The "watch in peace" release: no more ghost duplicates, no more getting kicked out of your own party, and the buffering finally gets treated honestly.

Fixes (every one root-caused from your reports, then locked)
- Duplicate "Lalabs Dazo" in the WATCHING list is gone for good: a network blip used to leave your old connection half-open on the server, so your reconnect got a SECOND seat - the same person stacked up until the room hit its 8-person cap and your gf got bounced with "That room is full" while you lost the host star. The server now treats your resume token as YOU: reconnecting replaces the ghost seat instantly (host role and all), half-open sockets expire after a quiet minute, and a replaced ghost's late close can never evict the live you. Both phones losing signal for a minute no longer ends the party either - the room waits for you
- No more stuck "CONNECTING": a failed rejoin used to leave the panel lying forever with sync frozen. Failed rejoins now retry with backoff and then clean up honestly; a seat swept mid-session repairs itself by resume token
- Watching different cuts (sub vs dub, or two servers after a hop) no longer turns sync into a fight: when the runtimes disagree, position sync goes HONESTLY approximate (big gaps only, never a seek into your own credits) and the party panel says so in plain words: "Different cut/audio - position sync is approximate. Pause and episodes still follow."
- "It keeps pausing" is fixed: a source that buffer-stalls used to relay its machine pauses to the WHOLE room. Only real user presses reach the room now (a press inside the provider's own player confirms after a beat - a stall that auto-resumes cancels itself), and your quick pause-then-play always lands instead of being swallowed by its own echo and force-reverted
- Switching server (mirror) now moves the WHOLE room: switch as host and everyone follows within a beat - same source, same cut, tight sync. The panel tells you when someone is on a different source and how to match it. (Full room-follow needs the hub update - see the note at the bottom)
- Joining a party - or reloading mid-episode and jumping back in - goes STRAIGHT to the video at the room's exact position. The "Continue playing / Start over" question never appears inside a party anymore (solo watching still gets it)
- VidBolt's ads can't hijack playback anymore: VidBolt now runs sandboxed first (its popups and modal ads are dead there), with one honest auto-retry without the shield if the player refuses the sandbox - and the engine label always tells the truth about which mode you're in. "Change server", "Try again", the ad's close button, Quality & Server and Audio & Subtitles are ALL clickable now - on desktop the mouse clicks straight through to the player, on phones/tablets the new hand button in the bar switches tap-through on (floating "Our controls" pill brings our bar back)
- VidBolt buffering gets real first aid: stalls used to "fix" themselves by reloading the whole player onto the SAME quality. Now a stall drops one quality step LIVE (from the level it's really running, not a blind ladder), re-priming the buffer in place. And the triage is honest: if your connection is fast and the server keeps stalling, we move you to a proven server instead of degrading your picture to 480p - and say which one it blamed. The Settings -> Servers list now shows every server's live status: down / "struggles here" / "fast for you" (measured on YOUR connection)
- Episode switching mid-party can't lurch the room anymore: no more seeking a fresh episode onto the old episode's timeline, and if a provider's own auto-next walks a participant off the room, they snap back to the room's episode instead of diverging

Quality of life
- "Reload player (keep position)" lives in the A/V sheet - one tap clears a stuck ad, overlay or error panel without losing your spot (beats F5)
- Manual "Switch Server" re-arms BOTH self-heal budgets - a burned health hop can never leave a title unhealable after you pick a new server

Honest limits
- Two different cuts (sub vs dub) can never be second-for-second in sync - we approximate and admit it instead of pretending
- In-iframe ads inside a third-party player can't be stripped by any website (browsers forbid it) - the sandbox kills their popups/tab-hijacks; Brave/uBlock still handles in-stream ads, and the A/V sheet's Reload button is the one-tap escape
- Room-wide source switching and the ghost-seat fixes speak the new hub protocol: the Cloudflare hub update ships with the next manual Worker deploy - until then the client-side protections (join-once-per-socket, rejoin recovery, teardown) are already protecting you
