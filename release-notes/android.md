The StreamBox Settings Redesign - a glass panel in the middle of your video, not a flat drawer on the side.

What's new
- The player's Settings (Playback / Subs / Servers) now pops up CENTERED over the video as a modern semi-transparent glass modal - the old solid side drawer is gone. The video stays faintly visible through frosted glass, with a soft teal glow, rounded 26px shell and a gentle pop-in animation (disabled automatically for users who prefer reduced motion)
- Redesigned from the ground up inside: segmented glass tabs with a gradient active state, pill buttons that lift on hover and press on tap, gradient highlight for every active choice (speed, sleep timer, languages, servers), refined uppercase section labels, glass inputs with focus glow, and a slim translucent scrollbar
- TV-remote friendly: every control keeps its full focus ring and keyboard flow; nothing moved functionally - same buttons, same behavior, new skin

Honest limits
- The redesign is pure presentation: no playback, subtitle or server behavior changed, and the QA gate now pins the modal's shape so it cannot silently revert to the old drawer
